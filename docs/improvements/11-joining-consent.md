# Joining disclosure and optional marketing consent

The joining notice and optional marketing preference demonstrate the MVP's consent controls. Before joining, the invited participant sees what the organiser and group can see, that card details stay private, and the seven-day retention and leaving policy.

The marketing checkbox starts unticked for each invitation. Joining works without selecting it. Checking it records only that account's demo preference and timestamp. No marketing messages are sent. Preferences can be reviewed or withdrawn under More → Privacy and data. Switching accounts or opening a fresh invitation resets the invitation checkbox to unticked.
